**Study title:** Early support for children's wellbeing: a pilot of the Kito

**Researcher:** `[STUDENT NAME]`, `[UNIVERSITY]`

**Ethics reference:** `[ETHICS REFERENCE]`

### What is this study about?

We want to find out whether a school platform can help staff notice when a child
is having a difficult time, and whether families find the information useful. The
platform brings together four kinds of information: how a child says they are
feeling, how their schoolwork is going, how they are getting on with others, and
their sleep, activity and daily habits.

### Why has my child been invited?

Every child in `[YEAR GROUP / CLASS]` is being invited. Taking part is voluntary.
Saying no will not affect your child's schooling, support or relationship with
staff.

### What would my child be asked to do?

- Complete a short daily check-in: four questions about mood, energy, sleep and
  feeling connected, plus an optional note. It takes about a minute.
- Log sleep, activity, screen time and breakfast, if they wish.
- Nothing else. There are no tests, interviews or observations for the study.

You would be asked to complete one short weekly pulse: two ratings and an
optional comment. Staff would record their usual observations.

### Who can see what?

- Your child can see their own check-ins and habits. They **cannot** see a score,
  an alert or a staff note about them.
- You can see your own child's page, your pulse history, and any update a mentor
  chooses to share with you.
- Staff assigned to your child can see the information they need to support them.
- The head and administrator see totals and trends for the school, not individual
  emotional notes, case notes or messages.
- Researchers see a pseudonymised extract. They do not see names or contact
  details.

### What are the possible benefits and risks?

Possible benefits: earlier support, better communication between home and school,
and a clearer picture of how your child is doing. Possible risks: a child may feel
watched, a score may be misunderstood, or a false alert may cause an unnecessary
conversation. The platform never labels a child as "at risk" in the child's own
view. You can raise a concern with `[SCHOOL CONTACT]` at any time.

### Do I have to take part?

No. You can say no now, or withdraw later, without giving a reason. Withdrawal
stops future collection. It does not remove records that have already contributed
to an anonymous total, and it does not override the school's safeguarding duty if
a member of staff has already acted on a concern.

### How long are records kept?

Raw records are kept for `[RETENTION PERIOD]` and then deleted. Anonymous totals
may be kept for the write-up. The school's safeguarding records follow the
school's own retention policy.

### Who do I contact?

- Questions about the study: `[STUDENT NAME]`, `[EMAIL]`
- Concerns about your child: `[SCHOOL CONTACT]`
- Independent concerns: `[UNIVERSITY ETHICS OFFICE]`
- Data protection: `[SCHOOL DPO]`

## Document 3 — Child assent (approximately ages 8–11)

**What is this?**

We are trying out a new school website called Kito. It asks how you are feeling
and how you are sleeping and playing. We want to know if it helps grown-ups help
you earlier.

**What would I do?**

You would answer four short questions each day. It takes about a minute. You can
also write a note if you want to. You do not have to write a note.

**Who sees my answers?**

Your teachers who look after you can see some of your answers. The head teacher
cannot see your private notes. Other children cannot see your answers. Your
grown-ups can see how you are doing.

**Will I see a score about myself?**

No. Kito does not show you a score or a label about you. If you want to know how
you are doing, you can ask `[TRUSTED ADULT]`.

**Do I have to do it?**

No. You can say no. You can stop whenever you want. Nobody will be cross with
you, and school will not change.

**What if I write something worrying?**

If you write something that makes an adult worried about your safety, they will
follow the school's safeguarding rules and try to help. They will not put your
words on a public page.

**What if I have a question?**

You can ask `[TRUSTED ADULT]` or `[STUDENT NAME]`.

## Document 4 — Child assent (approximately ages 12–16)

**Study title:** Early support for children's wellbeing: a pilot of the Kito
school platform

**What is the study?** We are testing whether a school platform helps staff notice
when things are getting difficult and offer support earlier. The platform looks at
four areas: how you feel, schoolwork, friendships, and sleep/activity/habits.

**What would I do?** A daily check-in of four 1–5 questions and an optional note
(about one minute), and a habit log if you choose. No interviews, tests or
observations.

**Who can see my information?** Staff assigned to support you; your parent or
guardian for your own page and shared updates; not other children. The head and
administrator see school-wide totals, not your private notes, case notes or
messages. Researchers see a pseudonymised extract.

**What I will not see.** You will not see a score, risk label or alert about
yourself. This is deliberate: a label can be harmful and you cannot easily
contest it. You can ask `[TRUSTED ADULT]` about your support at any time.

**Do I have to take part?** No. You can say no now or stop later, without giving a
reason. It will not affect your schooling, grades or support.

**What if I disclose something worrying?** Staff will follow the school's
safeguarding policy. They will try to help and will not publish your words. The
platform may raise an urgent internal alert for a trained adult, but it does not
store your note text on that alert.

**How long are records kept?** `[RETENTION PERIOD]`, then deleted. Anonymous
totals may be kept for the write-up.

## Document 5 — Staff information sheet (summary)

You are invited to take part in a pilot of the Kito platform. Participation means
using the platform as part of your normal pastoral work: recording structured
observations, reviewing alerts assigned to you, and communicating with consented
guardians through the platform.

- **What is collected:** your structured entries, alert actions and messages, plus
  an audit record of access. No performance monitoring is derived from this for
  your appraisal.
- **What you will see:** only the children currently assigned to you. You will not
  see another mentor's cases, and you cannot read a child's private note unless
  the child shares it.
- **What you will not see:** the administrator's configuration data, other
  families' threads, or the offline model benchmark (administrator only).
- **Support:** training on the safeguarding screen, the escalation route and what
  to do when a child discloses. A named safeguarding lead is available for urgent
  cases.
- **Withdrawal:** you can stop using the platform for the pilot at any time; your
  existing records remain part of the school's safeguarding record where the law
  requires it.
- **Contacts:** `[SCHOOL CONTACT]`, `[STUDENT NAME]`, `[UNIVERSITY ETHICS OFFICE]`.
