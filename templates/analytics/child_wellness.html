How this score is calculated

Each domain is first turned into a daily value between 0 and 1, then averaged over a {{ period_days }}-day window and multiplied by 100. The overall score applies these weights to whichever domains have data on a given day, renormalised so missing domains are not counted as zero. It needs at least two domains on that day; otherwise there is no overall score for that day.

DomainWeightWhat is measured
Emotional wellness{{ weights.0.percent }}%Mood, energy, sleep quality and felt connection (1–5 ratings)
Academic progress{{ weights.1.percent }}%Marks as a share of the maximum available
Healthy habits{{ weights.2.percent }}%Sleep, activity, screen time, nutrition and breakfast against age-appropriate guidelines
Social development{{ weights.3.percent }}%Peer relationships, participation, cooperation, conflict and belonging (1–5 ratings)

Confidence is about coverage, not certainty: good means the window contains enough reporting days for this domain's cadence, moderate means about half, and thin means one or two entries.
