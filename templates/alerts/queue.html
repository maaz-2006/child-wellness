How to read a tier

  • Urgent — two consecutive, well-covered weeks at a very low overall level, or a safeguarding signal in a note. Review today.
  • Concern — a sustained pattern, several domains low at once, or two difficult guardian pulse weeks. Review this week.
  • Watch — a downward trend, one domain below the child's own usual range, or a gap in recent entries. Keep an eye on it.

Tiers are not a clinical triage. They order attention; a person decides what happens next.
