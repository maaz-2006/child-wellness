What the counts mean

A rising open count is not proof that children are less well; it can mean recording has improved or a mentor is on leave. The useful operational question is whether every open item has an owner and whether unresolved items are getting older.

Urgent items are never auto-notified to a family. The designated safeguarding lead owns them, and the response is a person following the school's procedure.
